Pay Comparisons

USA Pay by Role

BLS OES May 2023 median annual wages. Entry = 10th pct, mid = 50th pct (median), senior = 90th pct.

Roles ordered from highest to lowest mid-career pay.

India Pay by Role

NASSCOM/MOSPI 2023 data, shown in US dollars two ways: **market USD** (paycheck converted at 82.6 INR/USD) and **PPP USD** (local buying power, ÷22 INR/PPP USD).

China Pay by Role

NBS 2023 Statistical Yearbook sector wages, shown in US dollars two ways: **market USD** (paycheck converted at 7.08 CNY/USD) and **PPP USD** (local buying power, ÷4.1 CNY/PPP USD).

Cross-Country Pay Comparison (USD)

Two dollar views:

  • **Market USD** — paycheck converted at the 2023 exchange rate (what they earn globally).
  • **PPP USD** — local buying power in dollars (already reflects cost-of-living differences).

PPP factors: USA 1.0, India 22 INR, China 4.1 CNY per PPP USD (IMF 2023).

Job Volume

USA Job Volume

Three views: tracked-role headcount, the sector mix *among tracked roles*, and the **economy-wide** sector mix (~161M workers, BLS CES 2023) showing software/IT's true share of all US jobs.

India Job Volume

Three views: tracked-role headcount, the sector mix *among tracked roles*, and the **economy-wide** sector mix (~500M workers, MOSPI PLFS 2022-23). Agriculture dominates, so software/IT is a tiny slice of all Indian jobs.

China Job Volume

Three views: tracked-role headcount, the sector mix *among tracked roles*, and the **economy-wide** sector mix (~740M workers, NBS 2023) showing software/IT against China's large manufacturing, trade, and agriculture workforces.

Cross-Country Job Volume

Software workforce size and share — first among the tracked roles, then **economy-wide** (software/IT as a share of each country's entire labor force).
